This Acceptable Use Policy (“**AUP**”) is part of the [Terms of Service](/terms). Capitalized terms follow the Terms. Operator and policy index: [/legal](/legal).

## 1. Push standards

Use AirSignal only for lawful, permissioned push. You must:

- Obtain and honor End User push permission under law and store rules.
- Identify yourself clearly and respect opt-out / OS notification controls.
- Keep content accurate and non-deceptive.
- Keep volume and relevance reasonable for your product.

## 2. Prohibited content

Do not use the Services for content or campaigns that are illegal, fraudulent, phishing, malware, harassing, hateful, exploitative of minors, infringing, spam, or otherwise barred by app-store rules.

## 3. Technical abuse

Do not bypass limits or security, probe or load-test without written approval, interfere with other customers, abuse free tiers, reverse engineer where prohibited, submit Sensitive Information without written agreement, or put send API keys in client apps.

## 4. Resale

Free and self-serve plans are for your own apps. Do not white-label AirSignal for unrelated third parties without a written agreement ([sales@airsignal.dev](mailto:sales@airsignal.dev)).

## 5. Enforcement

We may investigate, suspend, throttle, remove content, or terminate accounts for violations, and may report serious abuse.

## 6. Reporting

[support@airsignal.dev](mailto:support@airsignal.dev) or [legal@airsignal.dev](mailto:legal@airsignal.dev).
