This page applies only to **Enterprise** customers under a written Order Form. Free, Starter, and Pro are not covered. Related: [Terms](/terms) · [/legal](/legal).

## 1. Availability

We work to keep the AirSignal production API and dashboard available and reliable. We do **not** guarantee a specific uptime percentage, and we do **not** offer automatic service credits for downtime.

Push delivery also depends on third parties (for example Google FCM). Their outages or policy changes are outside our control.

## 2. What Enterprise includes

Unless your Order Form says otherwise, Enterprise may include:

- Custom MAU capacity and commercial terms agreed in writing
- Priority support via the channels named in your Order Form
- Reasonable advance notice for planned maintenance when practicable

Exact scope is always what your Order Form states.

## 3. Exclusions

We are not responsible for unavailability caused by scheduled or emergency maintenance, events beyond our reasonable control, third-party push or cloud providers, your configuration or rate limits, beta features, suspension under the Terms, or actions you or your agents take.

## 4. Custom terms

If you need a contractual uptime target or service credits, that must be negotiated and written into your Order Form. Nothing on this page creates those rights by itself.
