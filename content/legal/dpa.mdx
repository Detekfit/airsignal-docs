This Data Processing Addendum (“**DPA**”) is part of the [Terms of Service](/terms) (or other agreement) between AirSignal and the customer (“**Customer**”). If it conflicts with the Agreement on data protection, this DPA controls. Operator details: [/legal](/legal).

## 1. Roles

Customer is Controller (or Processor instructing us). We are Processor of **Customer Personal Data** — personal data we process for you in the Services. Account data of your team that we handle as controller is covered by the [Privacy Policy](/privacy), not this DPA.

Terms like Controller, Processor, and Personal Data follow UK GDPR / EU GDPR as applicable (“**Data Protection Laws**”).

## 2. Our obligations

We will:

1. Process Customer Personal Data only on your documented instructions (including the Agreement and product settings), unless law requires otherwise.
2. Keep staff who access it under confidentiality.
3. Maintain appropriate security measures (Annex B).
4. Help with Data Subject requests where reasonable given the nature of processing.
5. Notify you without undue delay after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data.
6. Delete or return Customer Personal Data after the Services end, subject to legal retention.
7. Make information available to show compliance and allow audits as in Section 6.

## 3. Your obligations

Provide lawful instructions, do not send special-category data unless agreed in writing, configure the SDK/API lawfully, and ensure you have a valid basis to process End User data.

## 4. Subprocessors

You authorize us to use Subprocessors listed at [/subprocessors](/subprocessors). We impose protective terms and remain responsible for them. If you object to a new Subprocessor on reasonable data-protection grounds within 15 days of posting, we will discuss in good faith; if unresolved, you may terminate the affected Services as sole remedy and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid unused fees (if any).

## 5. Transfers

Where we transfer Customer Personal Data from the UK/EEA to a country without adequacy, we use a valid transfer mechanism (for example SCCs / UK IDTA) unless another lawful basis applies.

## 6. Audits

Once per 12 months on written request (sooner after a breach or regulator request), we will provide a written security summary. On-site audits are by arrangement, under confidentiality, and at your expense if no material non-compliance is found.

## 7. Liability and term

Liability follows the Agreement except where Data Protection Laws forbid limitation. This DPA lasts for the Agreement and while we process Customer Personal Data.

## Annex A — Processing details

**Subject:** Hosted push-notification Services.  
**Duration:** Agreement term plus deletion/return.  
**Purpose:** Provide push, storage, analytics, and support you configure.  
**Data subjects:** Your Authorized Users (limited cases) and End Users of your apps.  
**Data:** As you submit — identifiers, device attributes, tags, notification content, engagement events.  
**Special categories:** Not intended.

## Annex B — Security (summary)

Access controls; protection of sensitive credentials; logical separation between customers; monitoring against abuse; backups appropriate to the Services; Subprocessor diligence. Detailed infrastructure design is not published here; Enterprise customers may receive more under NDA.
