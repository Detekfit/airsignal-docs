## 1. Who we are

AirSignal operates a hosted push-notification platform. Contact: [support@airsignal.dev](mailto:support@airsignal.dev).

## 2. Data we process

We process account data (name, email), organization membership, app configuration, API usage, device/subscriber metadata required for delivery (e.g. push tokens, device attributes), and billing-related identifiers provided by our payment processor (Paddle).

## 3. Why we process it

To provide the Service, authenticate users, deliver notifications, enforce plan limits, prevent abuse, and process payments/taxes via Paddle as Merchant of Record.

## 4. Processors

Payment and tax collection are handled by Paddle. Infrastructure and email providers may process data as subprocessors to operate the Service.

## 5. Retention

We retain account and operational data while your organization is active and for a limited period afterward for security, billing disputes, and legal obligations. Analytics events are retained according to your plan's retention window.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data by contacting [support@airsignal.dev](mailto:support@airsignal.dev).
