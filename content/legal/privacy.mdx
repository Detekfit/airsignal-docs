Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy describes how **AirSignal** (operated by Detekfit Ltd — “**we**”, “**us**”) handles personal information.

AirSignal is a hosted **push-notification** platform. Our Customers (“**Clients**”) add our SDKs or APIs to their apps so they can send and measure push notifications to their own users (“**Client End Users**”).

This Policy covers:

1. **Client End Users** — people who use a Client’s app that includes AirSignal  
2. **Clients and their team** — people who use the AirSignal dashboard, APIs, or billing  
3. **Website visitors** — people who use [airsignal.dev](https://airsignal.dev)

It does **not** cover a Client’s own products or websites. If you use an app that sends push through AirSignal, that app’s publisher is usually responsible for its own privacy notice. Ask them first about your data in their app.

Related: [Terms](/terms) · [Cookies](/cookies) · [DPA](/dpa) · [Subprocessors](/subprocessors) · [Legal](/legal)

## 1. Controller or processor?

| Situation | Our role |
| --- | --- |
| Push / subscriber data we process for a Client’s app | **Processor** (service provider) for the Client |
| AirSignal dashboard accounts, billing, support, and our website | **Controller** |

When we act as processor, the Client decides what to collect and why. Our [DPA](/dpa) applies to that processing.

## 2. Client End Users (SDK and APIs)

### What may be collected

When a Client uses AirSignal, we may process on their behalf:

- Push token and subscription / subscriber identifiers  
- Optional profile fields the Client sends (for example `externalId`, email, name, phone, country)  
- Device details the Client or SDK sends (platform, model, OS, app/SDK version, language, timezone)  
- Tags or aliases the Client sets  
- Activity used for delivery and analytics (for example sessions, heartbeats, notification opens)  
- Notification content and targeting the Client submits  
- Technical data needed to operate the Service (for example IP address on API requests)

We do **not** require precise GPS, contacts, photos, or End User payment cards. Clients must not send Sensitive Information unless we agree in writing.

More detail for developers: [Data collected by the SDK](/docs/privacy-and-security/data-collected-by-the-sdk).

### How we use it

Only to provide the Services to the Client — deliver push, show the Client analytics and audiences, enforce plan limits, secure the Service, and provide support. We do **not** sell Client End User personal information and do not use it to advertise AirSignal to those End Users.

### Who we share it with

Vendors needed to run the Services (for example push delivery via Google FCM, infrastructure, and support tools). See [Subprocessors](/subprocessors). We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the Service and people’s safety.

### End User choices and requests

- Use the app’s and device’s notification settings to stop push.  
- Contact the **app publisher** for access, deletion, or other privacy requests about data in that app.  
- We help Clients under the [DPA](/dpa) when they ask us to assist.

## 3. Clients and website visitors

### What we collect

- Account data: name, email, login method (password or OAuth), organization membership and roles  
- Billing metadata from our payment partner (Paddle) — not full card numbers  
- Support messages and optional chat content  
- Website usage if you accept analytics cookies ([Cookie Policy](/cookies))  
- Security and diagnostic logs

### How we use it

To run accounts and billing, provide support, improve and secure airsignal.dev and the Services, and meet legal duties. Marketing cookies run only with consent.

### Sharing

Same categories as above (payments, email, hosting, analytics/chat when consented). See [Subprocessors](/subprocessors). We do not sell this personal information.

## 4. Cookies

On airsignal.dev we use essential cookies and, with your permission, analytics and marketing cookies. Details and controls: [Cookie Policy](/cookies). Use **Cookie settings** in the footer anytime.

## 5. Retention and transfers

We keep account data while your organization is active and for a limited time after; billing records as required by law; and Client analytics per the Client’s plan. International transfers use safeguards described in the [DPA](/dpa).

## 6. Security

We use administrative and technical measures appropriate for a hosted SaaS. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object, or port personal information, and to withdraw consent.

- **Client End Users:** contact the app’s publisher first.  
- **Clients / website visitors:** [legal@airsignal.dev](mailto:legal@airsignal.dev) or [support@airsignal.dev](mailto:support@airsignal.dev).

You may also contact your data protection authority (for example the ICO in the UK).

## 8. Children

AirSignal is for businesses. We do not knowingly create dashboard accounts for children under 16.
