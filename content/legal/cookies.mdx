This Cookie Policy explains cookies and similar technologies on airsignal.dev. See also the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and [/legal](/legal).

## 1. Consent

On a first visit we show cookie preferences before non-essential tags run. You can **Accept all**, **Decline**, or **Manage preferences**. We store your choice in `airsignal_consent` for 12 months. Reopen anytime via **Cookie settings** in the footer or on [/legal](/legal). Non-essential tags stay off until you accept them.

## 2. Categories

### Essential (always on)

Needed to run the site and Services, keep you signed in, complete verification flows, remember this choice, and run checkout when you start a purchase (Paddle).

### Analytics (optional)

**Google Analytics** — helps us understand site usage. Loads only if you accept Analytics.

### Marketing (optional)

Google advertising features, if enabled. Loads only if you accept Marketing.

### Support chat (optional)

Live support on selected pages. Loads only if you accept Analytics. Decline keeps it off.

## 3. Managing cookies

Use **Cookie settings** on this site, your browser controls, or the [Google Analytics opt-out](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## 4. Changes

Updates are posted at [/cookies](/cookies). Questions: [legal@airsignal.dev](mailto:legal@airsignal.dev).
