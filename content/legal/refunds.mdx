This policy covers AirSignal subscriptions. Related: [Terms](/terms) · [Pricing](/pricing) · [/legal](/legal). Self-serve payments are processed by **Paddle** as Merchant of Record.

## 1. Digital goods — no general refunds

AirSignal is digital SaaS. Access starts when you purchase. **Sales are final.** No refunds for subscription fees, renewals, upgrades, or overage, except where law requires, for documented billing errors (below), or credits expressly written into an Enterprise Order Form.

## 2. Cancellation

Cancel from organization Billing → **Manage billing** (Paddle portal). Access continues through the paid period; cancellation stops future renewals and does not refund the current period. Downgrading to Free may reduce features and retention — export data first.

## 3. Overage

MAU overage charges are for usage already delivered and are non-refundable.

## 4. Billing errors

If a charge looks wrong (for example a duplicate), email [support@airsignal.dev](mailto:support@airsignal.dev) promptly. We investigate with Paddle case by case.

## 5. Enterprise

A signed Order Form overrides this policy where it conflicts.
