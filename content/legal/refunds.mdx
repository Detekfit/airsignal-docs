## 1. Digital goods — no refunds

AirSignal provides **digital goods and services** (hosted SaaS software and related plan entitlements). Because delivery is electronic and access begins immediately upon purchase, **all sales are final. No refunds apply** to subscription fees, renewals, upgrades, or usage/overage charges.

## 2. Cancellation

You may cancel a paid subscription from the organization Billing page via **Manage billing** (Paddle customer portal). Cancellation is scheduled for the end of the current billing period unless otherwise stated in the portal. You retain paid access through that period. Cancellation stops future renewals; it does **not** create a refund for the current or past periods.

## 3. Overage charges

MAU overage invoices for Starter/Pro are separate one-time charges for usage already delivered. Overage charges are non-refundable.

## 4. Billing errors

If you believe a charge was made in error (for example, a duplicate charge), contact [support@airsignal.dev](mailto:support@airsignal.dev) promptly so we can investigate with our Merchant of Record, Paddle. Corrections for documented billing errors are handled case by case and are not a general refund right.
