## How AirSignal works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an app">
    Each app is one push sender + audience, identified by a public `appId`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect subscribers">
    Your SDK or client calls `/v1` collect routes with `X-AirSignal-App-Id` to register devices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send notifications">
    Your backend calls `POST /v1/notifications` with a project REST key (`nsk_...`) to target
    subscribers, external ids, or your whole audience.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Collect (`X-AirSignal-App-Id`) and send (`nsk_...`) are two different credentials. Never mix
  them — see [Authentication](/docs/api/authentication).
</Callout>
