---
title: Create an app
description: Set up a new AirSignal app and get your public appId.
---

An **app** is one push sender plus its audience. You'll usually create one app per product or
per environment (e.g. separate apps for staging and production).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Go to [airsignal.dev/dashboard](https://airsignal.dev/dashboard) and select your organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the app">
    Click **New app**, give it a name, and choose **Google push (Messaging)** as the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your service account">
    Upload a Google service account JSON so AirSignal can send through FCM on your behalf. See
    [Get a Google service account JSON](/docs/guides/service-account) if you need to create one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your appId">
    Your new app has a public `appId` (`app_...`). SDKs and clients use this with
    `X-AirSignal-App-Id` for every collect call — see [Authentication](/docs/api/authentication).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">
  Project REST keys (`nsk_...`) are optional at this point — only generate one under
  **Settings** when you're ready to send from your backend. See [API keys](/docs/guides/api-keys).
</Callout>
