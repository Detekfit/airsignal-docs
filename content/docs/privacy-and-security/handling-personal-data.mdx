## Soft unsubscribe (push opt-out)

Public collect APIs support soft unsubscribe for a push subscription (the device stops receiving
push). This is the correct path for End User opt-out from the SDK.

Inactive or invalid push endpoints may also be unsubscribed automatically so you are not billed or
messaged against dead devices.

## Hard delete

Hard delete of subscribers is available in the **dashboard** for authenticated organization users.
There is no public hard-delete subscriber API for apps. Use the dashboard when an End User exercises
erasure and you choose to remove their AirSignal record entirely.

## Login / logout identity

- **Login** associates the current device with your `externalId` (creating the identified subscriber
  if needed).
- **Logout** returns the device to an anonymous subscriber and keeps the identified account; it
  does **not** by itself opt the user out of push.

## Retention

- Subscriber and subscription records remain while your organization/app is active, subject to your
  deletions.
- Analytics history follows your **plan retention window**.
- Leaving a paid plan may shorten how long analytics history is kept.

## Export

Use the dashboard and APIs to read the subscriber and subscription records you need before
cancellation or deletion. Enterprise customers can contact [support@airsignal.dev](mailto:support@airsignal.dev)
if an Order Form requires assisted export.
