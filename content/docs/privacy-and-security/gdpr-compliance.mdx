This page explains how **UK GDPR** and **EU GDPR** (EEA) apply when you use AirSignal for push.
It is practical guidance for Clients — **not legal advice**. For counsel-reviewed contracts, contact
[legal@airsignal.dev](mailto:legal@airsignal.dev).

Operator: **Detekfit Ltd** (United Kingdom). Policies: [Privacy](/privacy) · [DPA](/dpa) ·
[Subprocessors](/subprocessors) · [Legal](/legal).

## 1. Scope: UK and EU / EEA

| Law | Typical relevance |
| --- | --- |
| **UK GDPR** + Data Protection Act 2018 | Detekfit Ltd is a UK company; many Clients and End Users are in the UK |
| **EU GDPR** | Applies when you offer an app to people in the **EEA** or monitor their behaviour there |
| **ePrivacy** (and UK PECR) | Push permission / device storage and access — you must get valid notice and consent where required |

AirSignal’s [DPA](/dpa) is written to cover **UK GDPR and EU GDPR** processing of Customer Personal
Data. Market-specific addenda can be agreed in an Enterprise Order Form.

## 2. Roles

| Data | Your role (Client) | AirSignal role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| End User / subscriber data in your AirSignal app | **Controller** (or processor for your users) | **Processor** — [DPA](/dpa) |
| Your team’s dashboard accounts and billing | — | **Controller** — [Privacy](/privacy) |
| Cookies on airsignal.dev | — | Controller — [Cookies](/cookies) |

If you are an End User of a Client’s app, contact that app’s publisher first. We assist the Client
under the DPA.

## 3. What processing happens (push)

High-level flow for EU/EEA and UK End Users:

1. Your app or SDK registers a device / subscriber with AirSignal (collect APIs).  
2. You (or your backend) create a notification and choose an audience.  
3. AirSignal processes tokens and targeting data to deliver push (via Google FCM) and to show you
   delivery / engagement analytics.  
4. You remain responsible for the privacy notice, lawful basis, and End User rights for that app.

**Categories we may process for you** (only what you send or the Service generates for delivery):
identifiers (subscriber / subscription / push token), optional profile fields, device attributes,
tags/aliases, activity and notification events, and notification content you submit.

Details: [Data collected by the SDK](/docs/privacy-and-security/data-collected-by-the-sdk) ·
[Privacy Policy](/privacy) § Client End Users.

**We do not** decide your lawful basis, write your End User privacy policy, or replace your duty to
obtain push permission.

## 4. Lawful basis (your decision)

Common patterns for push (confirm with your counsel):

- **Consent** — device notification permission plus clear information (often required under ePrivacy /
  PECR for non-essential storage/access).  
- **Contract** — strictly necessary messages tied to a service the user requested.  
- **Legitimate interests** — only where appropriate and balanced; often weaker for marketing push.

Special-category data must not be sent to AirSignal unless agreed in writing and you have a valid
condition under GDPR Article 9 / UK equivalent.

## 5. International transfers

When Customer Personal Data moves from the **UK or EEA** to a country without an adequacy decision,
AirSignal uses a valid transfer tool as described in the [DPA](/dpa) (for example EU SCCs and/or
UK IDTA / Addendum), unless another lawful basis applies.

Vendors: [Subprocessors](/subprocessors). Hosting topology is not published publicly; Enterprise
buyers may request details under NDA.

## 6. Compliance plan for Clients (UK & EEA)

Use this as a practical checklist before you go live in the UK or EEA:

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Map what you send to AirSignal (fields, tags, events). |
| 2 | Update your **app privacy policy** and store listings; name AirSignal as a push provider and link [Privacy](/privacy). |
| 3 | Implement OS push permission and in-app notice / consent where required. |
| 4 | Accept AirSignal [Terms](/terms) (DPA incorporated) or sign an Order Form + DPA for Enterprise. |
| 5 | Set a process for End User rights (access / delete): use dashboard unsubscribe / delete; we assist under the DPA. |
| 6 | Avoid Sensitive Information in tags or payloads. |
| 7 | If you appoint an EU representative or DPO under GDPR, keep AirSignal contacts ready: [legal@airsignal.dev](mailto:legal@airsignal.dev). |
| 8 | Re-check after major SDK or data-field changes. |

Store form help: [Apple App Privacy](/docs/privacy-and-security/apple-app-privacy-requirements) ·
[Google Play Data Safety](/docs/privacy-and-security/google-play-data-safety).
