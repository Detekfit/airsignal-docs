## Product scope

Hosted **mobile push notifications**. Not an email/SMS/in-app messaging suite.

## Hosting

AirSignal is a company-operated hosted SaaS. We do not publish infrastructure providers, regions, or system design in public documentation. Enterprise customers may request details under NDA via [sales@airsignal.dev](mailto:sales@airsignal.dev) or [legal@airsignal.dev](mailto:legal@airsignal.dev).

## DPA

Yes — [DPA](/dpa) incorporated with the [Terms](/terms). See also [Subprocessors](/subprocessors).

## Security (high level)

We apply industry-standard administrative and technical controls appropriate to a multi-tenant SaaS, including access control, encryption for sensitive material, and monitoring for abuse. Detailed control lists are shared with Enterprise customers under NDA when required for procurement.

## Employee access

Limited to authorized personnel who need access to operate or support the Services, under confidentiality obligations.

## Breach notification

As described in the [DPA](/dpa).

## Data deletion

Customers can opt End Users out of push via the SDK/API and delete subscribers from the dashboard. Analytics retention follows the customer’s plan.

## Audits

Enterprise customers may request a security summary under the DPA; schedule via [sales@airsignal.dev](mailto:sales@airsignal.dev).
