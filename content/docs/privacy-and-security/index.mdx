AirSignal is a hosted **push-notification** platform operated by Detekfit Ltd. This section helps
developers and privacy reviewers understand what the SDKs and APIs collect, how GDPR roles work,
and how to complete Apple and Google store questionnaires.

<Callout type="info">
  These pages describe **customer-facing** data practices only. We do not document hosting
  providers, internal architecture, or security tooling in public docs. Enterprise procurement
  details are available under NDA.
</Callout>
